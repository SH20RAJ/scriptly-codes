# Doctor & Medical Practice — Professional Physician & Surgical Clinic Template

A premium, HIPAA-aware HTML5 web template designed for private medical practices, surgeons, specialized clinics, and healthcare consultants looking to establish profound patient trust and drive qualified consultation bookings.

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Authoritative Healthcare Aesthetics**: Deep surgical navy, sterile white, and clinical teal palette paired with elegant Playfair serif headers.
- **Interactive Appointment Booking Modal**: Multi-field scheduling form supporting in-person consultations, telehealth second opinions, and post-op reviews.
- **Specialties & Procedures Grid**: Structured sections to communicate complex treatments clearly.
- **Emergency Contact Top Bar**: High-visibility hotline and operating hours.
- **Board Credentials & Hospital Affiliations**: High-trust badges highlighting board certifications (FACS, American Board of Surgery).
- **Patient Reviews Section**: Real-world recovery testimonials and patient reassurance.
- **Pure HTML5 & Tailwind CSS**: No complicated framework setup—customizable in plain text and ready to deploy in seconds.

## 🚀 Quick Start
1. **Local Preview**:
   Double click `index.html` or run:
   ```bash
   npx serve .
   ```

2. **Customization**:
   - Replace clinic contact information, doctor credentials, and procedure descriptions in `index.html`.
   - Update appointment form action to point to your email handler, Formspree, or EHR system.

3. **Instant Deployment**:
   ```bash
   npx surge . my-clinic-practice.surge.sh
   ```
