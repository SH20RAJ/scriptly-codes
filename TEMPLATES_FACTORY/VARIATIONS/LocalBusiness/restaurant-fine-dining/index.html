The Culinary Vision

Rooted in Earth, Fire, and Deep Time

Executive Chef Henri Moreau curates a nightly 9-course sensory journey. Every vegetable is harvested that morning from our 14-acre organic bio-dynamic farm in Sonoma Valley.

From 48-hour koji-fermented broths to heritage dry-aged Wagyu smoked over white oak embers, our kitchen operates on respect for unadulterated flavor profiles.
