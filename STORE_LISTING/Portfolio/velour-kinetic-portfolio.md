# Velour — Kinetic GSAP Parallax Studio & Agency Portfolio

In a world of stagnant, template-driven agency sites, **Velour** commands immediate attention. Built around fluid GSAP physics and responsive 3D mouse parallax calculations, Velour provides the definitive first impression for modern creative studios, design ateliers, and digital innovators.

### 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

- **Kinetic Floating Portrait Hero**: Multi-layered 3D floating photo cards that respond continuously to cursor hover, mouse tilt vectors, and natural physics-based float loops.
- **GSAP ScrollTrigger Pipeline**: Orchestrated word-by-word reveal typography, letter drop staggered animations, and fluid entrance timelines.
- **Atelier Collective Grid**: Minimalist luxury team showcase with subtle hover depth transitions.
- **Animated Performance Metrics**: Kinetic number counters highlighting delivered projects, years of combined craft, and client satisfaction ratings.
- **Ultra-Luxury Obsidian & Solar Gold Design**: Tailored for high-end creative direction with fine grain textures, ambient radial light halos, and crisp contrast.
- **100% Self-Contained**: Pure vanilla JavaScript and GSAP CDN — zero npm build steps or complex framework dependencies.
