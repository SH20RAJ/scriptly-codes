## 💎 Long Description

### Elevate Your AI Product with Relentless Design Authority

Most AI SaaS products fail to convert because their landing pages look like generic boilerplate. Enterprise marketing leads and founders demand speed, clarity, and visual impact.

**Meet VETRA.**
Engineered with Next.js 15 App Router and Framer Motion, VETRA delivers a masterclass in modern digital marketing aesthetics:
- **Interactive Automation Visualizer**: Showcase complex multi-step AI triggers, campaign generation, and LLM reasoning steps directly on the page.
- **Dynamic Pricing & ROI Calculator**: Allow prospective customers to calculate estimated time and revenue savings dynamically.
- **Precision Bento Grid**: Modular sections highlighting sub-40ms execution, neural reasoning, and multi-channel marketing campaigns.
- **Dark Glassmorphism & Micro-Interactions**: Curated obsidian dark palette with glowing accents and smooth scroll physics.
- **Production-Ready**: Built on accessible Radix UI primitives with complete TypeScript typing.
