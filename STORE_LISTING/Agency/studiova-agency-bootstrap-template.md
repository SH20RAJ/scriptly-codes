### Long Description

# Studiova — Premium Creative Agency & Studio Bootstrap 5 Template

Stop losing premium clients to cookie-cutter, dated websites. **Studiova** is an ultra-modern, conversion-engineered Bootstrap 5 agency template created for top-tier creative studios, digital agencies, branding boutiques, and ambitious freelancers who demand world-class aesthetics.

Built on the latest Bootstrap 5 architecture with zero heavy framework bloat, Studiova delivers lightning-fast load times, seamless responsive layouts across all mobile and desktop devices, and refined micro-interactions.

---

### 🌟 Key Highlights & Features

- **12 Multi-Page Layouts**: Includes Home, About Us, Projects Grid, Project Details, Blog Journal, Blog Post, Contact Form, Sign In, Sign Up, Legal, and custom 404 pages.
- **Cinematic Video Hero**: Immersive hero header with background video reel support and bold typography.
- **Interactive Owl Carousel**: Smooth client portfolio showcase sliders with touch gesture support.
- **Animated Data Counters**: Showcase agency milestones, satisfied clients, and completed projects with dynamic number animation.
- **Modern Dark & Neon Aesthetic**: Curated high-contrast design system featuring #C1FF72 neon lime accents on deep onyx backgrounds.
- **Production-Ready & Well Documented**: Clean semantic HTML5, modular CSS custom properties, and commented JavaScript.
- **Zero Node.js Required**: Ready to drop into Netlify, Vercel, Surge, or any shared hosting cPanel immediately.

---

### 📦 Pages Breakdown

1. **Homepage (`index.html`)**: Video reel hero, services overview, featured work carousel, statistics counter, testimonials, and contact footer.
2. **About Studio (`about-us.html`)**: Agency philosophy, leadership team profiles, and brand history timeline.
3. **Selected Works (`projects.html`)**: Filterable project gallery with image hover effects.
4. **Case Study (`projects-detail.html`)**: Comprehensive project briefing, challenges, solutions, and deliverables gallery.
5. **Insights & Journal (`blog.html`)**: Clean editorial layout for thought leadership and industry commentary.
6. **Single Article (`blog-detail.html`)**: Formatted reading experience with author bio and social share controls.
7. **Contact Us (`contact.html`)**: Functional contact form, direct email links, and embedded map coordinates.
8. **Client Portal (`sign-in.html` & `sign-up.html`)**: Authentication views for client portals.
9. **Legal Suites (`privacy-policy.html` & `terms-and-conditions.html`)**: Standardized agency legal templates.
10. **Error Page (`404.html`)**: Engaging custom 404 layout.
